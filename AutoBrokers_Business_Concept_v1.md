## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**AutoBrokers** is a B2B/B2C automotive brokerage platform that simplifies vehicle acquisition for individuals, corporate fleets, and financial institutions. We act as full-service intermediaries, handling procurement, documentation, compliance, and logistics—eliminating friction from a highly fragmented market.

**Core Thesis:** Modern buyers (retail + corporate) demand transparency, speed, and hands-off convenience. Traditional dealership networks offer neither. AutoBrokers fills this gap using technology, data, and a lean operational model.

**Target Launch:** Q4 2026 (Europe, phased)  
**Initial Revenue Model:** Commission (3–6% per transaction) + value-added services  
**Projected Unit Economics:** 15–20% EBITDA at scale (500+ transactions/month)

---

## 1. MARKET OPPORTUNITY

### Market Size & Dynamics

- **EU Vehicle Sales (2026):** ~12M units annually (retail ~9M, commercial fleet ~3M)
- **Market Fragmentation:** Dominated by traditional dealer networks (70–80% of retail transactions); buyer experience consistently rated poor (satisfaction ~6.5/10)
- **Emerging Gaps:**
  - Retail: lack of price transparency, opaque financing, slow end-to-end process (30–60 days)
  - Corporate: fleet acquisition complexity, vendor consolidation, compliance overhead
  - Cross-border: regulatory friction, currency/VAT uncertainty, no unified procurement platform

### Competitive Landscape

| Player | Model | Scope | Weakness |
|--------|-------|-------|----------|
| Traditional Dealers | Retail sales, financing | Local | No transparency, slow |
| Vroom / Cazoo | Direct retail (B2C) | Limited inventory | Low scale, high burn, no B2B |
| Mandataire Auto (FR/DE) | Broker / mandatary | Regional | No tech, limited scale, no B2B |
| Corporate Fleet Mgmt (Arval, LeasePlan) | Leasing, fleet services | B2B corporate | Lease-heavy, expensive, lock-in |
| Direct Marketplaces (Autoscout24) | Listings only | Classified ads | No transaction facilitation |

**AutoBrokers Differentiation:** Full-stack brokerage (procurement + compliance + logistics) + B2B + transparent pricing + cross-border capability.

## 2. BUSINESS MODEL

### Revenue Streams (Phased)

#### Phase 1: Commission (Core)
- **Retail:** 4–5% of sale price (buyer or seller commission, or split)
- **B2B Corporate:** 3–4% of fleet value + volume discounts
- **Example:** €25k vehicle → €1,000–1,250 per transaction

#### Phase 2: Value-Added Services (Year 2)
- **White-label brokerage:** Dealerships / leasing companies use our backend (1–2% rev share)
- **Extended warranties / insurance:** 8–12% commission on ancillary products
- **Fleet management software:** SaaS subscription (€50–200/fleet/month)
- **Financing facilitation:** 0.5–1.5% referral fee to finance partners

#### Phase 3: Data & Intelligence (Year 3+)
- **Market pricing indices:** Subscription for dealers, insurers, fleets
- **Used car evaluation AI:** Valuation API / SaaS
- **Predictive analytics:** Buy/sell timing recommendations (B2B)

### Unit Economics (Mature State)

| Metric | Retail | B2B Corporate |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| Avg Transaction Value | €25k | €120k (fleet avg) |
| Commission Rate | 4.5% | 3.5% |
| Gross Revenue/Deal | €1,125 | €4,200 |
| Cost per Deal* | €280 | €450 |
| Gross Margin | €845 (75%) | €3,750 (89%) |

*Includes procurement, compliance review, doc processing, logistics coordination

**Path to Profitability:**
- Break-even: ~120 transactions/month (€135k MRR gross, ~€60k fixed ops)
- Contribution margin: 89% (highly scalable post-PMF)

- Consolidated procurement (single invoice, multi-vendor)
- Compliance automation (VQF/KYC/VAT for cross-border)

**Partnerships:**
- Finance: Klarna, PayPal Credit (buy now, pay later)

**New User Acquisition:**
- Referral from finance partners (co-branded offers)

## 6. REGULATORY & COMPLIANCE

### Jurisdictional Considerations

**Switzerland (CHE):**
- VQF registration required (Oxen's existing infrastructure)
- Motor vehicle brokerage license (Maklerpatent, cantonal level)
- AML/KYC for payments >€10k (Banking Act)

**Germany (DE):**
- Makler license (IHK registration) or partnership with licensed dealer
- Registration via KBA (Kraftfahrtbundesamt)
- VAT on brokerage services

**France (FR):**
- Registered broker status (Courtier d'Assurance or Motor Broker)
- Regulation by ORIAS if handling escrow

**Cross-Border:**
- VAT reverse charge for B2B fleet sales
- Country-of-origin registration guidelines
- Green card (EU insurance proof)

**Advantage:** Escrowfy's existing VQF infrastructure de-risks compliance setup.

## 8. KEY SUCCESS FACTORS & RISKS

### Success Factors
1. **Regulatory clearance** (VQF, cross-border licensing) — Escrowfy advantage
2. **Dealer partnerships** (inventory access at scale) — direct sales
3. **Unit economics** (keep ops cost <€300/deal) — lean operations, automation
4. **Customer acquisition** (SEO, partnerships) — organic growth initially
5. **Net Promoter Score** (target >50) — brand differentiation vs. dealers

### Key Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Dealer resistance to disintermediation | Partner model (commission share, not replacement) |
| Regulatory complexity (cross-border) | Escrowfy's VQF + legal team |
| Working capital (escrow float) | Bank relationships, factoring if needed |
| Market saturation (Cazoo, others) | Superior UX + B2B expansion |
| Inventory sourcing | Long-term dealer agreements, B2C marketplace fallback |
| Customer acquisition cost | Partnerships + referral loops vs. paid ads |
